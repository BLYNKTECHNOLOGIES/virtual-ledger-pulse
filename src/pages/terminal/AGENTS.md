# Copilot decisions (v2 Playbook)

- Copilot AI runs only for Copilot approvers (copilot_config.approver_ids, checked by copilot_is_approver) and only for the order chat an approver has open: the chat panel calls copilot-draft, nothing runs in the background, because AI spend and Playbook training are limited to chats the approvers handle.
- copilot_my_access feeds the screen; every Copilot gate in the UI reads it and copilot-draft / copilot-learn check again, because screen and server must agree.
- Approved Playbook replies (copilot_match_playbook, scoped by situation, side, small/regular, account and client state; older replies by status) are shown before any AI and skip drafting; the most specific fitting reply per question wins, then the one used in the most similar chat; one chat state (last message, status, situation) is drafted once (copilot_requests.request_key), so two approvers never pay twice for the same message.
- Sending or approving a suggestion saves it to the Playbook through copilot-learn ("use = approve"; Undo within 24 h, only while it is the entry's latest change); own typed replies only when Save is on; rejected drafts are kept as examples to avoid, never as replies.
- No reply text carries a typed number: numbers come from copilot_contacts through {contact:<team>} (teams are rows of copilot_teams; available-now first), order values through {amount} {orderNo} {name} {upi}; guardDraft holds back release promises (English and Hinglish), outside-Binance offers, typed numbers and, on our sell orders before verification, anything that invites payment; the composer refuses to send an unfilled placeholder, because a client must never see a wrong number or a placeholder.
- A number is given only when the client asks for one: the KYC team's to a new client still in verification (the one-time verification call is the KYC team's, never the relationship team's), the Relationship team's once KYC is done.
- Personal data in the chat (phones, UPI IDs, account numbers, PAN, Aadhaar, references) is masked with maskPII before any AI call and in stored questions.
- Language and address are decided on the server from the chat: Hindi in English letters only after the client keeps writing Hindi past hindi_switch_after English replies; "ma'am" only when the client refers to herself as female, never from a name.
- The Copilot never skips or suppresses KYC; it shows the client's KYC status and the rule comes from the Playbook.
- Operators get no AI and never change the Playbook; copilot_config.operators_enabled stays false until an approver switches on approved replies (a separate step).
- Tentative entries (the one-time seed, source 'seed') are offered to approvers only and stop being offered after tentative_days unless used; they must never reach operators, because nobody has approved them.
- The Playbook page (/terminal/playbook) and its sidebar item exist only for approvers (copilot_my_access); inbox decisions go through copilot_inbox_decide and copilot_resolve_conflict, so an entry and its inbox item always change together.
- The situation (what the client is waiting for: copilot_situations codes such as sell.kyc.pending, sell.kyc.call, sell.pay.ready, buy.pay.cooling) is worked out on the server from Binance status and events and our scripted messages (_shared/copilot-situation.ts). An approver's change (copilot_set_order_situation) holds until our next step; client messages never undo it, and the Binance-status situations (marked paid, completed, cancelled, appeal) cannot be changed, because replies are chosen by situation.
- KYC happens outside the chat, so a step worked out from our messages (the KYC steps; on buy orders details, paying, beneficiary activation) is a best guess: copilot-draft also offers the matched question's replies for the neighbouring steps (alternativeSteps, stepOptions; only replies that do not also apply at the current step) and names steps with no reply of their own for a one-tap switch. "Client can pay" is never offered before Binance's Verified notice or our payment details, and no reply that invites payment is offered as another step before verification. A reply sent unchanged for another step sets the chat to that step once Binance has the message (copilot_set_order_situation from the delivery callback, so the mark is newer than our message) and is saved under that step (copilot-learn situation, never out of KYC); the step to set is the one the chat will show after sending, when it is one of the reply's steps, because the approver knows the client's real step.
- A question (copilot_playbook_topics) owns its phrasings; each reply covers situations through scope.stages, where a group code (sell.kyc) covers its steps, including steps added later, so approved replies are re-pointed, never thrown away, when situations change. A reply whose situation code no longer exists is never offered until one is picked again.
- Each approver send or approve saves the masked chat before the reply as an example (copilot_playbook_examples, latest 12 per reply) and adds the client's wording to the question; replies are ranked by how closely the current chat matches their examples, and the saving judge sees them to tell a variant (different situation) from a conflict (same situation).
- Editing one language copy marks the other out of date (entries trigger); an out-of-date copy is never offered (the other language is shown with a note) until it is rewritten from the other language or confirmed.
- Phrasings and example chats without an embedding are embedded in the same call as an approver's next chat lookup, after an approver's save, or when a question is opened on the Playbook page (copilot-learn index); there is no background embedding job.
- The old Copilot (copilot-suggest, copilot-teach, copilot-train, style exemplars, blacklist, hourly mining) is retired; its tables are kept as history and nothing reads them.
